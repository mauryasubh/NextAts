Frequently Asked Questions

Can I invite external stakeholders to review candidates?

Yes! On the Growth plan, you can invite external guest reviewers with read-only access to specific jobs for free. They don't take up full team seats.

Do I need a credit card to start the trial?

No. We don't ask for a credit card until your 14-day trial concludes. If you choose not to upgrade, your account safely reverts to the free Starter tier.

What happens to data if we downgrade?

If you drop to the Free tier, we will safely archive candidates beyond the "2 Active Jobs" limit. You can unlock them anytime by upgrading.
